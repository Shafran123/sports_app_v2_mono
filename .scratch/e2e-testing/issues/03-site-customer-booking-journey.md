# 03 — Site Customer booking journey

**What to build:** The full guest-to-booked journey on the Dedicated Site: land on the site home from `mysite.localhost`, open Smash Arena's venue page, pick a court and slots on a seeded day, reach checkout, create an account through the real site sign-in form, complete cash checkout (auto-confirm on), and land on a confirmed booking with its QR shown.

**Blocked by:** 01

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Site home lists the seeded venue; venue page shows courts, prices, and open hours.
- [ ] A guest selects a court and slots; checkout reflects seeded pricing.
- [ ] Sign-in at the confirm step creates a real Site Customer account (the one real-form spec).
- [ ] Cash checkout completes and the booking is confirmed with its QR code shown.
- [ ] The booking exists on the backend for the Demo Business (data-level check).
