# 0024 — QR token in player booking emails

- **Status:** accepted
- **Date:** 2026-08-24

## Context

Booking confirmation, reminder, and bill emails to the player should carry the single-use check-in QR code ("QR Token", CONTEXT.md) so the player has it in their inbox at the venue gate. The bill already emails the QR inside the attached PDF (since the first implementation). Today the QR is never in email bodies.

## Decision

Player-facing booking emails (confirmation, reminder, bill) embed the QR as an **inline CID image** (`<img src="cid:booking-qr.png">`) via the existing multipart/FormData transport attachment path, generated by the existing `qrcode` dependency. The **owner/admin emails never carry it** — the token stays out of the shared booking payload and out of the fan-out to other roles.

The shared `bookingLoader` payload continues to exclude `qr_token`; instead a dedicated `loadQrToken(bookingId)` is called only by the player-email builder path after asserting the recipient is the booking's player.

## Consequences

- QR disclosure broadened: player's **own transactional inbox** (confirm/reminder/bill) + their app — already tracked in CONTEXT.
- One inline PNG per affected email (≈ 1-2 KB); bill keeps PDF + gains inline QR.
- The existing `emailService.sendEmail` path grows `attachments: []` handling (`attachment` shim preserved).
- `emailTemplates.js` grows shell preheader/venue row/CTA/wordmark plus the player booking builders; preview harness renders all 16 under `.scratch/emails-preview/` for human acceptance.

## Comments

- The event emails deliberately carry no QR (event registrations have no venue check-in token today; the plumbing exists later), per grill Q18.
- Player-cancel email carries no QR (booking is over; refund info is the content), per grill decision.
- Dark-mode email styles (Q9 "yes"): implemented as `@media (prefers-color-scheme: dark)` counter-overrides in the shell, not a separate build.
